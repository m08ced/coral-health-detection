Coral Health Detection

An AI-based tool for detecting and assessing the health of hard corals. Created by Cantalejo, Coritico, Dizon

How to use: Upload a single underwater coral reef image (JPG or PNG). The system will automatically detect hard corals and classify each as alive or dead. Non-hard coral regions will also be identified. Results appear below with annotated bounding boxes.
